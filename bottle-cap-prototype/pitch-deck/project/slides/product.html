The product

Powder sealed in the cap

1

Press

An X-shaped blade cuts the foil seal. A spring pulls it back out, so the opening is clear.

2

Shake

About 10 seconds. The full dose mixes into 500 mL of water.

3

Lift

Drink and train. The cap screws off and back on like any bottle.

Pop Off

Hand investors the prototype here. Pressing it themselves is worth more than any slide. Pop Off is a 500 mL bottle of water with a full dose of pre-workout sealed dry in the cap. Press it: a blade cuts the foil and the powder drops in. Shake for ten seconds and drink. A spring pulls the blade back out so all the powder gets through.
