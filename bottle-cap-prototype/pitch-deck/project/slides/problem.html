The problem

Two ways to take pre-workout. Both fall short.

Powder tub · $1.00–1.60 a serving

Full dose, full hassle

You carry it, scoop it and spill it in your gym bag, or forget it at home.

Canned drink · $2.00–3.33 a can

Convenient, but compromised

Mixed months before you drink it, so ingredients that break down in water, like creatine, are often left out.

Pop Off

Pre-workout users have two choices. Powder is cheap and full strength, but messy, and you have to carry it: picture the scoop spilling all over your bag. Canned pre-workout is convenient, but it is mixed months before you drink it, so brands leave out ingredients that break down in water. Creatine is the clearest example.
