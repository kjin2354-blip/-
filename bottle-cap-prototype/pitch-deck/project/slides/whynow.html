Why now

Single-serve is where the market is moving

Economics

Single-serve stick packs passed $2B milestone in US sales, up 52% in a year.

Social

Pre-workout has moved from bodybuilders to everyday gym-goers; the category grows ~7–9% a year.

Technological

Creatine breaks down in liquid, so a dry-until-use cap matters. 3D printing lets us prove it cheaply before buying molds.

Political · legal

Supplement labels follow FDA rules (DSHEA). We use a standard formula with standard claims, no new approvals needed.

Environmental

Single-use plastic is under pressure. Plan: rPET bottle and recyclable PP cap.

So what?

Buyers already pay for single-serve. Nobody offers it at full dose. That gap is open now.

Sources: BevNET 2025 (stick packs); Mordor Intelligence, Market Intelo (category growth)
