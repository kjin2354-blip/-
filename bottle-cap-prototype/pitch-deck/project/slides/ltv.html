Pop Off

A gym-pickup subscriber earns about 20 dollars a month and is worth about 165 dollars over eight months. Winning a customer costs 30 to 50 dollars, so we get back 3 to 5 dollars for every dollar spent. Investors look for 3 to 1. Our pilot will measure the real cancellation rate.
